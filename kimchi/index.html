The story

In deep,
on purpose

Deep Kimchi started as a folding table at the Saturday farmers market: one cooler, forty jars, and a hand-painted sign. The jars sold out by nine. People kept asking what to eat it with. So we started cooking.

We're a Korean kitchen with a plate-lunch heart. Halmoni's kalbi marinade, grilled over kiawe. Meat jun the way it's only made in Hawaiʻi. BBQ chicken because every plate lunch counter needs it. Kimchi fried rice with Spam because, honestly, it's better that way. Two scoops rice and your four sides on every plate, the way you grew up with.

Everything on the menu is built around one jar of kimchi we've been perfecting for eleven years.

We ferment in small batches in a walk-in held at 38°F. Napa from Waimānalo when we can get it, local won bok when we can't. Gochugaru we import ourselves. No vinegar, no sugar to fake the funk. Just salt and time.

Takeout only. A pickup window, a case of sides, a shelf of jars, and a bench outside. That's the whole restaurant.

Questions

Ask us
anything

What's the difference between mini and regular?

Regular is the full portion of meat, two scoops rice, and four sides. Mini is a smaller portion of meat, one scoop rice, and two sides. Mini is a solid lunch for one person. Regular is dinner, or lunch plus leftovers.

What sides can I pick?

House kimchi, cucumber kimchi, radish kimchi, bean sprouts, watercress, spinach namul, choy sum, mac salad, potato-mac salad, fish cake, pickled daikon, japchae, and kimchi slaw. The case changes a little day to day. If you don't pick, we'll give you kimchi, sprouts, watercress, and mac.

Can I order ahead?

Please do. Build your order on this page, then text or call it in. We'll reply with a pickup time. During lunch rush, texting is faster than calling. Orders over 8 plates need an hour of lead time. Fundraiser plates and party trays need more.

Do you have a kamaʻāina discount?

Yes. 10% off with a Hawaiʻi ID, military ID, or for kūpuna 65 and up. Every day, no minimum, no coupon. Just tell us at the window.

Is there anywhere to sit?

Not really. We're takeout only, with a bench out front and a couple of stools by the window. The park is close if you want to eat outside.

How spicy is "spicy"?

Our scale runs 0 to 3. Most plates land at 1 or 2. Anything can be made mild by swapping in white kimchi and skipping the gochujang. Level 3 items are marked, and yes, we'll go past 3 if you ask.

Vegan or gluten-free?

Yes. Our house kimchi is made without fish sauce or shrimp, so it's fully vegan. Look for the 🌱 and GF tags. Kalbi, bulgogi, and BBQ chicken marinades use shoyu, so they aren't gluten-free. Meat jun, fish jun, katsu, and mandoo contain wheat.

Do you deliver?

Not for individual orders yet. Kimchi Club subscriptions, fundraiser orders over 100 plates, and party trays can be delivered anywhere on Oʻahu.

Can I bring my jar back?

Yes please. Rinse it, bring it, get $2 off the next one. We sanitize and reuse every jar that comes back.
